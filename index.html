Quality Management Under Strain

The quality control process, managed by AECOM, is facing significant challenges. While many issues are raised, a substantial number remain unresolved, including critical structural and material defects that could impact long-term safety and durability.

Critical Open Issues:

  • Improper Pile Load Test: A potential defect in the deep foundation system of Clinical Block CN-03.
  • Uncertified Road Materials: Use of materials without proper Material Test Certificates (MTCs), a breakdown in quality control.
  • Façade Stability Concerns: Changes to Hilti stone clamps are affecting the stability of the building's exterior.
  • Sky Bridge Protocol: Lack of an approved protocol for constructing the critical sky bridges connecting buildings.

The "Smart Hospital" Vision at Risk

A key project goal is the creation of a digitally integrated "smart hospital." However, delays in finalizing interior MEP components threaten to compromise or force the de-scoping of these critical, efficiency-driving systems.

Key Automated Systems:

  • Pneumatic Tube System: 107 stations for rapid transport of lab samples, records, and medications.
  • Automated Waste & Laundry System (AWLS): Improves hygiene and reduces manual labor for waste and linen transport.
  • Building Management System (BMS): Centralized control of MEP systems for energy efficiency.
  • Asset Management System: Real-time tracking of critical medical equipment.
